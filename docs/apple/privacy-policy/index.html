Privacy policy

RetroRPN is an Apple Watch calculator made by Ashley Feniello. Your calculator data stays on your watch.

What the app saves

RetroRPN saves your calculator state on your watch, including the stack, the number you’re entering, and your four programmable functions. Deleting the app removes that saved state.

Data collection and sharing

The app has no account and sends no information to a server. RetroRPN collects no analytics, shows no ads, and does not sell or share information.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email RetroRPN support.
