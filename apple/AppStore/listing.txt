Description

RetroRPN is a reverse Polish notation calculator for Apple Watch, made in the spirit of the HP-35 and the other classic Hewlett-Packard machines.

A number goes on the stack, then the operation. There is no equals key. The stack is four levels deep, X, Y, Z, and T, and a small arrow shows when the number you are typing is still open.

It is a complete little scientific calculator.

• Arithmetic, powers and roots, logarithms, and sine, cosine, and tangent, in radians
• Reciprocal, absolute value, integer part, sign change, and a random number
• The constants e and pi
• Stack tools: enter to duplicate, swap, over, roll up, and roll down
• Store and recall
• Four programmable functions, a through d. Record the keys you press, then play them back the way you would use any built-in operation
• Undo and redo from the Digital Crown, one keystroke at a time

Starting a recording, or running one of those functions, finishes the number you are typing. You can enter a value and run the function directly.

RetroRPN is the classic calculator, on your wrist.
