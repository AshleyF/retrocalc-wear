# RetroRPN Privacy Policy

RetroRPN is a calculator for Apple Watch. Ashley Nathan Feniello makes it.

The app stores your calculator on the watch: the stack, the number you are typing, and the four programmable functions. That information stays on the device. RetroRPN does not have an account, and it does not send the calculator, or anything else, to a server.

RetroRPN does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell or share information.

Deleting the app removes the saved calculator.

Last updated November 9, 2026.
